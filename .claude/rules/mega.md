# /mega game quirks (the rules are in mega.py's top comment)

- "Daily level limit met" shows only after a different Pokémon was Mega Evolved.
- The page right after a Mega Evolution won't swipe, so reopen from the list.
- While a Mewtwo rests, the other form's tab is greyed out.
- The energy icon can read as a leading digit (915 for 15).
- Tell pages apart by weight/height, never CP (Chandelure's flames change the CP read).
- Not yet seen live: the iPhone X tap on "Mega Level increased!", a real YES after the box_key wait, the 5 s pass, whether SHOW ME can open the level page.
- The badge's "HH:MM left" is the Mega's timer only up to 08:00. Over that ("22:35 left") it's the rest timer's last day (2026-11-18). Not yet seen live: whether the level page says REST PERIOD on that last day, and what it says during a Mega.
